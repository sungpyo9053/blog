# 피지컬 AI 홈 이미지

- 용도: 홈의 주제 정체성을 표현하는 개념 일러스트. 실험 증거가 아님.
- 방식: imagegen 스킬의 기본 내장 image_gen 사용. CLI/API fallback 미사용.
- 산출물: `deploy/wordpress/huntlab-warm-editorial/assets/physical-ai-hero.png`
- 원본: Codex generated_images에 보존하고 프로젝트에 복사함.
- 검토: 로봇 팔, 카메라, 블록, 경로가 식별됨. 본문 글자는 HTML로 분리.
- 공개 화면에서 AI 생성 개념 일러스트이며 실제 실험 사진이 아님을 표시.

## 최종 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: editorial hero illustration for HuntLab, a Korean educational Physical AI blog. Create a refined wide landscape editorial illustration of a small articulated robotic arm with a camera observing a single geometric block on a simulation grid, with a restrained translucent perception field and a simple curved trajectory suggesting perception, planning and action. Clearly illustrative, not a real laboratory photograph and not evidence of a test. Clean tactile 3D plus precise technical drawing aesthetic, spacious light neutral background, controlled dark graphite and teal accents, readable silhouette at mobile size. No people, no humanoid face, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no numbers, no fake dashboard or performance metrics. Balanced composition that can be placed next to separate HTML headline text. Polished calm scientific learning publication, not neon sci-fi.
